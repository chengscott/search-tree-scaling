Search trees

The trees below were recorded from pUCT searches on Atari games. Each simulation descends from the root, choosing at every state \(s\) the action with the highest pUCT score (right): the value estimate \(Q(s,a)\) plus an exploration bonus that scales with the coefficient \(c\), the policy prior \(P(s,a)\) and the visit counts \(N(s)\) and \(N(s,a)\). A larger \(c\) spreads simulations over more actions, so the tree grows wider and shallower. More search threads do the same implicitly, since threads descend before earlier results are backed up. Choose a game, simulation count, thread count and \(c\) to browse the recorded trees.

\[ a^{*} = \arg\max_{a}\; Q(s,a) + c \, P(s,a)\, \frac{\sqrt{N(s)}}{1 + N(s,a)} \]

Provenance and method

Public build. The first 128 recorded steps of each of the 1109 archived settings (141,952 trees) are served from this site. The remaining steps (2,334,698 trees, 3.7 GB in total) load on demand from 5 static data mirrors when you move the step slider; nothing is fetched until you ask for it.

Recorded trees: every archived search step in the first recorded episode, starting with the original SGF __FullTree. Step 0 matches the original Figure 1 rendering script. The step index is zero-based; identical indices across different settings may represent different game states. Parent links, action, visits, policy and Q are archived data.
